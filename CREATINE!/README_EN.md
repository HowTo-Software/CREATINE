# CREATINE! - Build 42.20.2

CREATINE! adds creatine, a sports-bottle smoothie, and an intentionally over-the-top progression system based on weight, Fitness, and Strength.

## How it works

1. Find creatine in gym lockers, pharmacies, dry-food kitchens, and medical or military storage.
2. Consume it directly or use a sports bottle containing at least 0.5 L of water to prepare a Creatine Smoothie.
3. Add up to four ingredients. The original selection of fruit, vegetables, dairy, sweets, seeds, eggs, and meat remains supported.
4. Take a dose on seven different world days. Multiple doses on the same day count only once.
5. After loading, maintain the required weight, Fitness, and Strength to unlock each milestone.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Weight | Fitness | Strength | Benefits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Bully | 85 kg | 5 | 5 | Nutritionist, Thick Skinned, and Resilient |
| Tactician | 95 kg | 6 | 7 | Graceful, Gymnast, and Hunter |
| Berserker | 110 kg | 8 | 9 | Adrenaline Junkie, Athletic, and Axeman |
| Unyielding Will | 130 kg | 10 | 10 | Second Wind and Bulldoze |

Second Wind prevents a potentially fatal situation, restores the character, clears nearby threats, and then recharges for seven in-game days. Bulldoze kills zombies directly in the character's path while running.

Milestones are periodically reevaluated. If their requirements are no longer met, only traits granted by this mod are removed.

## Junkiez compatibility

The optional `CREATINE_JunkiezCompatibility` module requires both `CREATINE` and `JunkiezMoreSupplements`. It adds Junkiez supplements to the Creatine Smoothie and creatine to Junkiez shakes without replacing the original item definitions.

## Technical compatibility

- Minimum version: Project Zomboid 42.20.2.
- Single Player, Host, and dedicated server.
- Does not alter saves, maps, or Java classes.
- It can be removed without world conversion; its custom items and traits remain unavailable while disabled.
