CREATINE! — Mod Synopsis
description=
description=CREATINE! is a long-term fitness progression mod for Project Zomboid Build 42.20. It turns body weight, Fitness, and Strength into a unified character-development system where the player can deliberately build a massive, combat-oriented survivor.
description=To activate the system, the survivor must consume Creatine on seven distinct in-game days. Creatine can be consumed directly or added to Junkiez Shake and Junkiez Blended Shake recipes from More Workout Supplements. Once fully “loaded,” the mod continuously evaluates the character’s weight, Fitness, and Strength. Reaching increasingly demanding milestones grants cumulative trait packages and special abilities.

description=Unyielding Will-
description=Unyielding Will is the final achievement and unlocks two exclusive CREATINE! traits:
description=Second Wind activates when health reaches—or incoming damage would reduce it to—20% or less. It prevents or interrupts the critical hit where possible, restores the survivor to 35% health, briefly protects them from further damage, and kills nearby zombies within three tiles. It recharges after 72 in-game hours.
description=Bulldoze kills zombies directly in the survivor’s path while running. It operates within roughly 1.5 tiles and a forward-facing cone, turning a fully developed character into a literal human battering ram. Running still carries its normal stamina demands.
description=
description=Milestones-
description=The milestones are cumulative. A survivor who reaches Unyielding Will also qualifies for the preceding three packages. However, the requirements remain active: losing enough weight, Fitness, or Strength removes any milestone that is no longer supported. The mod carefully records which traits it granted so it does not remove traits the character possessed independently.
description=Once the seven-day loading period is complete, the normal Obese and Overweight traits are suppressed. This allows the survivor to carry substantial body mass as functional weight without being treated like an ordinary overweight character.
description=
description=Mod Compatibility-
description=More Workout Supplements is required. Creatine powder can be consumed directly or added to its Junkiez Shake and Junkiez Blended Shake recipes.
description=
description=Fitness Workout Overhaul compatibility: CREATINE! evaluates the character’s actual vanilla Fitness and Strength perk levels instead of depending on a particular exercise implementation. Training performed through Fitness Workout Overhaul therefore contributes naturally toward the milestone requirements. There is no hard dependency or direct FWO-specific code in the archive, which should let both systems coexist without CREATINE! replacing or interfering with FWO’s workout mechanics.
description=
description=In short: load Creatine for seven days, eat enough to build mass, train Fitness and Strength, and progress from an imposing survivor into a 130-kilogram, max-stat force capable of refusing death and running directly through the undead.
